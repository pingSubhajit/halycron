## Overview

Halycron supports albums with stricter handling to reduce accidental exposure and improve privacy—especially when you’re browsing quickly or sharing.

At a high level:
- **Sensitive albums** are treated as high-privacy collections.
- **Protected albums** add an extra access step (e.g., PIN) in the product UX.

This is separate from encryption: encryption protects bytes/filenames at rest; album sensitivity adds **product-level controls**.

<Note>
Album sensitivity controls are defense-in-depth. They do not replace encryption, device security, or safe sharing practices.
</Note>

## What sensitivity protection is for

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Reduce accidental exposure" icon="eye-off">
    Minimize inadvertent display (e.g., in general galleries, quick previews, or notifications).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Add friction for high-risk content" icon="shield">
    Require an explicit step before viewing or sharing.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Common behaviors (intended UX)

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Library views">
    Sensitive content may be hidden from default feeds and only shown inside the album after explicit access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sharing">
    For sensitive/protected albums, sharing may require a PIN and should prefer PIN-protected share links.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Notifications and previews">
    Avoid showing filenames or thumbnails in notifications for sensitive content.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Interaction with E2EE

- Encryption ensures the server can’t read photo bytes or filenames.
- Album sensitivity determines **when the client shows decrypted content** and what UI affordances are required.

## Recommendations

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use PIN-protected shares for sensitive albums">
    Non-PIN links are bearer links. For sensitive content, prefer PIN shares and treat the PIN as an additional barrier.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use device security as part of your protection model">
    Enable device lock screen, biometrics, and OS updates, especially for sensitive albums.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review share links periodically">
    Revoke links when no longer needed, and avoid long expiry windows for sensitive content.
  </Step>
</Steps>
